# Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra Fresnel Lens Cutting Guide

This project documents how to cut a custom fresnel lens for the Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra 3D resin printer using a CO2 laser cutter.

## Overview

The Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra uses a large fresnel lens to focus the UV light from the LCD screen onto the resin build surface. If your lens becomes damaged or scratched, you may need to cut a replacement.

## Materials Needed

- Fresnel lens sheet material (typically acrylic or PMMA)
- CO2 laser cutter with appropriate bed size
- Masking tape (optional, to protect lens surface)
- Cleaning supplies (isopropyl alcohol, microfiber cloth)

## Specifications

### Lens Dimensions
- **Outer dimensions**: Approximately 218mm x 123mm (verify with your specific model)
- **Thickness**: ~2-3mm
- **Material**: Clear acrylic or PMMA fresnel lens sheet
- **Groove spacing**: Varies by lens type (typically 0.2-0.5mm pitch)

### Laser Settings

**Note**: These are starting settings and may need adjustment based on your specific laser cutter and material.

- **Power**: 10-15% (start low to avoid melting)
- **Speed**: 100-150 mm/s
- **Frequency**: 1000-2000 Hz
- **Passes**: 1-2 (single pass preferred to minimize edge distortion)

## Cutting Process

### 1. Prepare the Design File

Create a vector file (SVG, DXF, or AI format) with the exact dimensions:
- Measure your existing lens carefully
- Include any mounting holes or notches
- Ensure corners are properly radiused if applicable

### 2. Prepare the Material

1. Apply masking tape to both sides of the fresnel lens sheet (optional but recommended)
   - This helps protect the grooved surface from smoke residue
   - Makes cleanup easier after cutting

2. Place material flat on the laser bed
   - Ensure material is completely flat (no warping)
   - Use weights or clamps if needed

### 3. Focus the Laser

1. Set the laser focus to the surface of the material
2. Use the laser's auto-focus feature if available
3. For manual focus, use the focal distance specified by your laser manufacturer

### 4. Test Cut

**Always do a test cut first!**

1. Cut a small sample piece with your initial settings
2. Check for:
   - Clean edges without melting or charring
   - Minimal heat-affected zone
   - No cracking or stress marks
3. Adjust power/speed as needed

### 5. Final Cut

1. Load your design file into the laser cutter software
2. Position the cutting path on your material
3. Double-check alignment and material placement
4. Run the cut
5. Monitor the first few seconds to ensure proper cutting

### 6. Post-Processing

1. Remove masking tape carefully
2. Clean any residue with isopropyl alcohol
3. Inspect edges for quality
4. If edges are rough, carefully sand with very fine sandpaper (2000+ grit)

## Installation

1. Clean the new lens thoroughly
2. Remove the old lens from the Saturn 4 Ultra
3. Install the new lens, ensuring correct orientation (grooved side typically faces down)
4. Secure any mounting hardware
5. Test the printer with a small print to verify proper UV exposure

## Safety Considerations

- **Ventilation**: Ensure proper ventilation when cutting acrylic/PMMA
- **Fire Safety**: Never leave the laser cutter unattended during operation
- **Eye Protection**: Use appropriate laser safety glasses
- **Material Safety**: Verify your material is laser-safe (never cut PVC or materials containing chlorine)

## Troubleshooting

### Melted or Rough Edges
- Reduce power setting
- Increase cutting speed
- Check laser focus

### Incomplete Cut
- Increase power slightly
- Decrease cutting speed
- Add a second pass

### Cracking or Stress Marks
- Material may be too thin or poor quality
- Reduce power to minimize thermal stress
- Ensure material is properly supported during cutting

## Notes

- Always verify dimensions with your specific Saturn 4 Ultra model before cutting
- Keep the grooved side of the lens clean and free from scratches
- Store spare lenses flat in a protective sleeve
- Consider cutting multiple lenses at once to have spares
